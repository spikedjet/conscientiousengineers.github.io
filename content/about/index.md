Conscientious Engineers is a community of engineers committed to building technology with purpose, ethics, and responsibility.

## Our Mission

We believe that engineering decisions carry real-world consequences. Our goal is to foster a community that approaches technology thoughtfully and shares knowledge through articles and discussions.
