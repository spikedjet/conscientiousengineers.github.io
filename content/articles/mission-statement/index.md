## What is the Collective of Conscientious Engineers?

We are a collective of engineers, scientists, designers, and developers who believe that what we design matters, that the product of our labor should be used to benefit all who are affected by it, and that we are the intellectual base which skillfully designs the means of production. We believe that the work we do for a living has immense implications for all, from small product development to major infrastructure projects, and that we must do all we can to ensure that our work does not lead to societal failure in the forms of environmental degradation, aiding imperialist forces, degrading our freedom, and creating more crises on top of every other crises we already need to address. We believe that access to information should be free, that we should know to what end our work is used for, and what goals our employers and the structure surrounding them are pursuing. We believe that engineers have an important role to play in the necessary societal change we must pursue to give everyone a chance at a good life, not just the few. We believe that we have enjoyed a privileged position in the middle class of society, and that we must use that position of privilege to level the playing field, to ultimately eliminate the class dynamic entirely. Lastly, we believe in organizing our workplaces so that we can leverage our power as the developers of the technology we specialize in to pursue these goals outlined.

Individually we are weak, collectively we are immensely powerful.
